## 1. Eligibility & User Verification

1. **Student Status:** The Platform is intended exclusively for verified undergraduate and graduate students, placement candidates, placed alumni seniors, and accredited campus placement cell moderators.
2. **Account Binding:** Users sign in via Google OAuth. To participate in submissions, coin transactions, and peer unlocks, students must bind a verifiable institutional email (`*.ac.in`, `*.edu.in`, `*.edu`) and their unique college registration/roll number.
3. **Anti-Spoofing:** You warrant that your roll number and institutional credentials are your own. Impersonation of another student or submitting fraudulent placement credentials will result in immediate permanent account termination.

---

## 2. User Content & Prohibited Materials (Non-Disclosure & Anti-Cheating)

1. **Own Words Requirement:** All Online Assessment (OA) summaries, question descriptions, and interview feedback must be drafted entirely in your own words.
2. **Absolute Prohibitions:** Users are strictly forbidden from uploading or posting:
   - Direct screenshots, photographic captures, or screencasts of live assessment windows.
   - Proprietary source code belonging to recruiting firms or assessment testing providers (e.g., HackerRank, Codility, TCS iON, Mettl).
   - Confidential questions subject to legally binding signed employer Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
   - Defamatory remarks, profanity, harassment, or confidential personal data of interviewers.
3. **Student Warranty:** By submitting an OA report or interview reflection, you warrant that you are not violating any active non-disclosure agreement or university placement code of conduct.

## 3. Placement Drive Embargo Policy

1. **Anti-Leak Embargo:** Companies frequently evaluate campus candidates across multiple successive batches or test windows. To protect academic integrity and placement cell contracts, **reports associated with an OPEN placement drive remain under cryptographic embargo**.
2. **Embargo Restrictions:** During an active drive, question text and statements are suppressed across all public feeds, searches, and exports.
3. **Circumvention Penalties:** Any attempt to leak, bypass, or scrape embargoed test materials will result in immediate reporting to the student's institutional placement cell.

## 4. Virtual Platform Currency (Coins)

1. **No Monetary Value:** "Coins" are virtual platform reputation points used solely to incentivize peer contributions and pace access. Coins possess zero monetary cash value, cannot be sold, transferred, or exchanged for fiat currency or prizes.
2. **Give-to-Get Privilege:** Any student who submits an approved contribution for a company unlocks that company's historical OA archive for free, permanently.
3. **Caps & Fair Play:** Daily post limits (5 submissions/day) and daily upvote earnings (50 coins/day) are strictly enforced server-side. Tampering with client headers or spoofing coin balances will result in balance revocation.

3. **Takedowns:** Rightsholders and employers may request expedited removal in accordance with our [Content Takedown Policy](file:///docs/TAKEDOWN.md).
